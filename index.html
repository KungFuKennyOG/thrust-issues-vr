Desktop: click the scene to play (Esc for this panel). Mouse look · WASD move · LMB/RMB fire left/right arm · hold Q/E to charge left/right, release to blast · hold Shift = thruster stance (arms point down-back) · Space jump · R reset.
VR: trigger = machine gun · hold grip to charge, release to blast · left stick move · right stick turn · A jump · X reset · hold Y to tune (right stick ↑↓ select, ←→ change).
